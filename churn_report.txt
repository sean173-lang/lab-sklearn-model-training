CUSTOMER CHURN PREDICTION REPORT

Approach:
Loaded and cleaned customer data, encoded categorical features, scaled measurements, and trained KNN.

Accuracy: 74.73%
Precision: 52.53%
Recall: 50.00%
Best K in test comparison: 11

Findings:
The model identifies patterns associated with churn. Contract type, tenure, and monthly charges should be investigated as possible churn indicators.

Recommendations:
Use predictions to prioritize customer retention outreach and evaluate whether interventions reduce churn.

Limitations:
KNN depends on scaling and may make incorrect predictions. Feature importance has not been established. Future improvements include cross-validation, class imbalance analysis, and model comparisons.
